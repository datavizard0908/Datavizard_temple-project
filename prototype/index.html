    第一章Chapter 1

    神明的故鄉

    Where the gods came from

    台灣民間信仰的主要神明，原鄉多在福建與廣東。清代渡海來台的移民，常帶著家鄉廟宇的香火同行，祈求航程平安，也在新土地上凝聚同鄉。

    Most of Taiwan’s major folk deities come from Fujian and Guangdong. Qing-era migrants often carried incense from their hometown temples, praying for a safe crossing and binding their communities together in a new land.

    對岸亮起的光柱，是各位神明的祖廟所在地。

    The beams of light across the strait mark each god’s ancestral temple.

    泉州人帶來的神明

    Gods from Quanzhou

    同安人的保生大帝、安溪人的清水祖師、南安人的廣澤尊王。看一位神明落腳在哪裡，常常就能推測當地早期的移民來自何方。

    Tong’an people brought Baosheng Dadi, Anxi people brought Qingshui Zushi, and Nan’an people brought Guangze Zunwang. Where a god settled often tells you where a place’s early migrants came from.

    漳州人的開漳聖王

    Zhangzhou’s Kaizhang Shengwang

    唐代開發漳州的將領陳元光，被漳州移民奉為祖籍神。宜蘭平原與台北的士林、內湖一帶，都是漳州人較集中的地方。

    Chen Yuanguang, the Tang-dynasty general who developed Zhangzhou, became the ancestral god of Zhangzhou migrants. The Yilan plain and Taipei’s Shilin and Neihu were Zhangzhou strongholds.

    客家人的三山國王

    The Hakka Three Mountain Kings

    三山國王是粵東潮州一帶的山神，隨客家與潮州移民來台，分布在桃竹苗、屏東六堆與宜蘭。

    Mountain gods from the Chaozhou region of eastern Guangdong, brought by Hakka and Chaozhou migrants to Taoyuan, Hsinchu and Miaoli, the Liudui area of Pingtung, and Yilan.

    第二章Chapter 2

    沿著河長出來的台北

    Taipei grew along its rivers

    鏡頭降落台北盆地。早期移民沿淡水河、大漢溪、新店溪與基隆河上溯，在河港上岸，聚落和廟宇也沿著河岸長出來。艋舺、大稻埕、新莊都是這樣起家的。

    The camera lands in the Taipei Basin. Early migrants sailed up the Tamsui, Dahan, Xindian and Keelung rivers and came ashore at river ports, and settlements and temples grew along the banks. Bangka, Dadaocheng and Xinzhuang all started this way.

    紅色牌匾標著「3D」的是代表性大廟，點進去可以看建築、建廟年份與遷移故事。

    Red plaques marked “3D” are landmark temples. Open one to explore its architecture, founding year and migration story.

    1853 年，連神明也要搬家

    1853: even the gods had to move

    艋舺的三邑人與同安人爭奪碼頭，爆發「頂下郊拚」。落敗的同安人從火中搶出霞海城隍的神像，先退到大龍峒，再到大稻埕落腳，1859 年建成霞海城隍廟。

    Sanyi and Tong’an people in Bangka fought over the docks in what became known as the Dingxiajiao Fight. The defeated Tong’an people rescued the Xia-Hai City God’s statue from the flames, retreated to Dalongdong and then to Dadaocheng, and completed the Xia-Hai City God Temple there in 1859.

    大稻埕後來因茶葉貿易興盛，反而比艋舺更繁華。

    Thanks to the tea trade, Dadaocheng later grew even richer than Bangka.

    第四章：意外的比較Chapter 4: Surprising comparisons

    每 100 間廟，有幾間是土地公？

    Out of every 100 temples, how many belong to the Earth God?

    土地公沒有單一原鄉，哪裡有聚落、哪裡有田，就有一間守護那片土地的小廟。地圖上還亮著的，就是土地公廟。

    The Earth God has no single homeland: wherever there is a village or a field, there is a small shrine guarding it. The lights still glowing on the map are Earth God shrines.

    數字為示範資料。Figures are demo data.

    北中南東，各拜哪位神？

    North, central, south, east: who do they worship?

    柱子越高，該區的廟越多；每一段顏色是一位神明所占的比例。

    The taller the column, the more temples in that region. Each colored band is one god’s share.

      數字為示範資料。Figures are demo data.

      廟比便利商店多嗎？

      More temples than convenience stores?

      紅柱是廟宇數，木色柱是便利商店數。在南部幾個縣市，廟反而比便利商店還多；只有都會區的便利商店明顯勝出。

      Red columns are temples; wooden ones are convenience stores. In several southern counties temples outnumber convenience stores. Only in the big cities do the stores clearly win.
